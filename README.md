# Saved Index

A Claude plugin by Munkherdene (https://www.instagram.com/mgsmp4/).

It turns your Instagram saved posts into a private visual board:

- **You pick the categories.** Claude looks at a sample of your saves and suggests a set of categories. You rename, merge, delete or add until it's yours, and then every post is sorted into it.
- **Grid**: your categories as covers, then every post, with search by caption, creator or hashtag.
- **Map**: every thumbnail on one zoomable canvas, grouped by your categories and arranged by colour or by newest. Click any image to open the post on Instagram.
- **Private by default**: the board is published as a private page in your Claude account. Share the link only if you choose to.

## How to use

Say "build my saved index". Claude will:

1. Open your Instagram Saved page in the browser you've connected to Claude (you log in yourself) and scroll it slowly, collecting the thumbnails, creators and captions the page shows.
2. Suggest categories based on what you actually save, and wait for you to edit and approve them.
3. Sort every post into your categories by looking at it.
4. Publish the board.

Later:

- "Add my new saves to the index": collects only what you've saved since last time.
- "Rename / merge / split my categories" or "add a category for X": updates the board without starting over.

## What you need

- Claude with a browser it can use (the Claude desktop app's built-in browser, or Claude in Chrome), logged in to your Instagram account.
- About an hour for 5,000–7,000 saves, mostly unattended.

## Good to know

- Claude only reads your Saved page. It never likes, unsaves, comments, follows, messages, or types your password.
- This is automated reading of your own account. Instagram's terms restrict automated collection, so there is a small risk of a temporary "try again later" limit. The collector goes slowly and takes breaks, and it stops if Instagram shows a warning.
- The thumbnails and captions belong to the creators you saved. Keep the board private, or share it only with people you trust.
